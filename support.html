Help & Safety Center

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our primary school tutoring, safety protocols, and subscription plans.

How are tutors background-checked and vetted?
Every tutor undergoes rigorous police background checks (DBS/criminal record checks), degree verification from top universities, and live trial teaching audits before conducting sessions.
Is BrightSparks 100% ad-free and COPPA compliant?
Yes! We strictly comply with COPPA & GDPR child privacy regulations. There are zero third-party advertisements, zero external popups, and zero data tracking for children.
What age and grade levels does BrightSparks cover?
We specialize in Primary School education covering Kindergarten through Grade 5 (Ages 5 to 11).
How does the 7-Day Free Trial work?
Your free trial gives full access to all micro-lessons, daily quizzes, and parent analytics. You will not be charged if you cancel before the 7 days expire.
